# Rechner

## Plan your pay and family finances.

Rechner brings together clear estimates for German take-home pay and parental-leave planning. Explore how salary, tax settings, family benefits and work plans can affect household cash flow—then inspect the assumptions and sources behind each result.

[**Open Rechner**](https://hkhademian.github.io/Rechner/)

### Calculators

- [**Net Pay Calculator**](https://hkhademian.github.io/Rechner/calculators/net-pay/) — estimate German take-home pay and see the tax and social contribution breakdown.
- [**Parental Leave Planner**](https://hkhademian.github.io/Rechner/calculators/parental-leave/) — compare parental-leave, work and household-income scenarios over time.

### Designed for clarity

- **See what shapes the estimate.** Results distinguish payroll withholding, standardized benefit calculations, annual tax assessment and cash received.
- **Follow the evidence.** Statutory assumptions link to their sources, and unavailable or projected calculations are identified.
- **Keep control of your inputs.** Calculations run in your browser. Choose whether calculator data lasts for the current page, browser tab or is saved on this device.

These tools support planning and learning. Estimates depend on the information supplied and the model's stated coverage; they are not official decisions or individualized tax or legal advice.
