# Contributing to Rechner

Rechner is proprietary, source-available software under the [Rechner Personal Use License](LICENSE). The hosted calculators are free for individual personal, non-commercial use. Source-code use, modification, redistribution, commercial use, and service provision require the permission described in the license.

## Feedback and bug reports

Use GitHub issues to report problems, suggest improvements, or discuss a calculation. For legal or financial calculation reports, include the relevant primary source, effective date, and an independently worked example when possible. Do not include real personal or financial data.

## Code contributions

Do not submit code or other changes unless the Maintainer has first authorized the work in writing. Accepted contributions require a signed [Contributor License Agreement](CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md) before they can be merged. It grants the Maintainer exclusive rights of use in the contribution, including commercial use and relicensing. German law does not allow copyright itself to be transferred during an author's lifetime; the agreement grants exclusive rights of use as permitted by law, while statutory authorship rights remain with the contributor.

Please keep discussion considerate and constructive. Security-sensitive reports should not be posted in public issues.
