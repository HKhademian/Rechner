# Child Benefit & Tax-Allowance Favorability

Pays tax-free monthly Kindergeld per qualifying child and automatically tests annually whether Kinderfreibetrag yields greater tax relief.

## Benefit

Guarantees monthly cash flow plus automatic optimization at higher marginal tax brackets.

## Limitation

You do not keep both full Kindergeld and full Kinderfreibetrag income-tax relief; § 31 nets them against each other.
