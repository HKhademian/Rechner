# EU Blue Card, Opportunity Card & Permanent Settlement

Governs skilled worker salary floors, shortage/graduate exemptions, Opportunity Card points, and accelerated permanent residency.

## Benefit

Enables 21- or 27-month permanent residence and streamlined spousal work rights when Blue Card thresholds are met.

## Limitation

Thresholds scale annually with statutory pension BBG limits and require careful salary verification at renewal.
