# Assessment Lookback for Later Births

Recomputes the 12-month assessment before each later birth, skipping maternity and eligible older-child Elterngeld months up to 14 life-months.

## Benefit

Prevents older-child leave months (within 14 life-months) from depressing the next child's pre-birth average.

## Limitation

Part-time work or unpaid leave outside the 14-month exclusion window reduces the assessment base for the next child.
