# Annual Joint Assessment & Progression Settlement

Reconciles monthly payroll withholdings against actual annual joint liability including splitting tariff and progression on replacement benefits.

## Benefit

Income splitting and deductions often generate substantial tax refunds when one partner has low/zero earnings.

## Limitation

Elterngeld and maternity progression (§ 32b) frequently create year-end tax repayments when one spouse earns full salary.
