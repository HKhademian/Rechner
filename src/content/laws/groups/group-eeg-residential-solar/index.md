# Residential Photovoltaic & Green Energy Incentives

Combines 0% VAT purchase relief, full income tax exemption up to 30 kWp, and 20-year statutory EEG grid feed-in tariffs.

## Benefit

Zero VAT upfront plus tax-free self-consumption savings (~37 ct/kWh) and guaranteed 20-year feed-in income.

## Limitation

EEG feed-in rates degress 1% semi-annually and export alone earns lower per-kWh returns than self-consumption.
