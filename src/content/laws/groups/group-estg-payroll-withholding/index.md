# Monthly Payroll Withholding & Bonus Rules

Governs monthly wage tax, single-parent relief, Class IV factor, Solidarity Surcharge, and differential bonus withholding via BMF PAP.

## Benefit

Accurate monthly cash flow modeling matching certified German payroll outputs.

## Limitation

Monthly withholding is only an advance payment; annual assessment determines final liability.
