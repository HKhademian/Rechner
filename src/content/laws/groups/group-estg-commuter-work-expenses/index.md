# Commuter Allowance, Homeoffice & JobTicket Rules

Governs daily commuter distance deductions, Homeoffice flat-rate relief, the €1,230 employee lump sum, and tax-free employer transit subsidies.

## Benefit

Combines tax-free employer transit benefits or excess Werbungskosten above €1,230 to reduce annual tax liability.

## Limitation

Tax-free § 3 Nr. 15 JobTicket subsidies reduce claimable commuter distance deductions euro for euro.
