# Standardized BEEG Net & Personal Plus Ceiling

Computes BEEG assessment net using flat social rates (9%/10%/2%) and January PAP taxes, independent of actual payroll take-home.

## Benefit

High replacement up to 100% at lower incomes and 2-for-1 duration stretching under ElterngeldPlus.

## Limitation

Capped at €2,770 assessable net (€1,800 Basis / €900 Plus) with no social-contribution ceiling in flat deductions.
