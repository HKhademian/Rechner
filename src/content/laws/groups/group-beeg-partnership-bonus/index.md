# Parent Claim Budgets & Partnership Bonus

Coordinates individual/family Basis & Plus budgets (12+2 months) and unlocks 2–4 Partnership Bonus months when both parents work 24–32h/week.

## Benefit

Adds up to 4 extra ElterngeldPlus months per parent when sharing work and childcare symmetrically.

## Limitation

Strict 24–32h corridor and minimum 2-month claim duration mean mistimed hours can forfeit bonus months.
