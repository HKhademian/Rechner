# Family Home Acquisition, KfW 300 & Closing Taxes

Evaluates federal-state real estate transfer taxes, KfW 300 subsidized family loan limits by child count, and renovation craftsmanship tax credits.

## Benefit

KfW 300 provides up to €270,000 low-interest family loans while § 35a refunds up to €1,200/yr in renovation labor tax.

## Limitation

Upfront closing taxes (3.5%–6.5% GrEStG + notary/agent) require liquid equity and KfW 300 tests prior-year zvE.
