# Household Sibling Bonus (Geschwisterbonus)

Grants a +10% supplement (minimum €75 Basis / €37.50 Plus) while qualifying older siblings remain under age 3 (or two under age 6).

## Benefit

Rewards closely spaced births with guaranteed minimum monthly additions.

## Limitation

Expires immediately after the older sibling passes the age threshold and requires known sibling ages.
