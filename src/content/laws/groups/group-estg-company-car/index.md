# Company Car & Electric Mobility Taxation

Models imputed benefit-in-kind for private vehicle use (1% ICE, 0.5% PHEV, 0.25% EV up to €100,000) and commute distance surcharges.

## Benefit

Pure electric vehicles within the €100,000 cap reduce monthly imputed taxable income by 75%.

## Limitation

Longer commutes add 0.03%/km monthly (quarter/halved for qualifying EVs/PHEVs) unless switched to actual-trip records.
