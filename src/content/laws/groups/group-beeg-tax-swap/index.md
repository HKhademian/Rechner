# Tax Class & Assessment History

Selects 12 eligible pre-birth months (skipping maternity/older-child exclusion months) and locks in the tax class held in the majority or tie-break of those months.

## Benefit

Planning a timely tax-class switch into Class III for the primary leave-taking parent can materially increase standardized Elterngeld net.

## Limitation

Late switches do not rewrite the majority/tie rule, and Class V reduces the other spouse's monthly paycheck until annual filing.
