# Maternity Protection & Reference Pay

Protects employment around birth and replaces 100% of the prior 3-month average net wage, credited against concurrent Elterngeld.

## Benefit

Preserves 100% net take-home pay during maternity protection and protects later BEEG lookbacks via exclusion months.

## Limitation

Post-birth maternity weeks consume mother's Basiselterngeld months and enter annual § 32b tax progression.
