# Social Security Contributions & Midijob Corridor

Models statutory health, pension, unemployment, and care insurance contributions including Midijob relief, Saxony care rules, and cent rounding.

## Benefit

Captures Midijob contribution savings and multi-child long-term care discounts accurately.

## Limitation

Childless employees aged 23+ pay a 0.6% surcharge, and Saxony employees pay an extra 0.5% base share.
