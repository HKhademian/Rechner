# Statutory Pension Insurance Rate (RV)

Sets the statutory pension contribution rate (18.6%, shared 9.3% employee / 9.3% employer) up to the statutory pension ceiling.

## Benefit

Builds statutory retirement entitlements and is 100% tax-deductible under § 10 EStG.

## Limitation

Represents the largest single social deduction on gross pay up to the pension assessment ceiling.
