# Annual Tax Credits & Signed Settlement

Credits withheld monthly wage tax and solidarity surcharge against final assessed annual tax liability, yielding a refund or repayment.

## Benefit

Returns excess monthly withholding (e.g. from temporary Class V during § 2b optimization) after filing.

## Limitation

Produces a tax repayment bill when monthly withholding (e.g. Class III or § 32b years) was lower than final liability.
