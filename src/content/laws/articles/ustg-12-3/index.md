# 0% VAT Rate on Residential Solar PV & Storage

Reduces German value-added tax from 19% to 0% for delivery and installation of residential solar panels, inverters, and home batteries.

## Benefit

Saves 19/119 (~15.97%) off standard gross hardware and installation invoices immediately at purchase.

## Limitation

Applies strictly to systems installed on or near residential/public buildings; commercial-only sites remain at 19%.
