# Solidarity Surcharge Levy Scope

Establishes the Solidarity Surcharge (Solidaritätszuschlag) levied on assessed and withheld income tax.

## Benefit

Most middle-income earners are fully exempt thanks to § 3/§ 4 SolzG exemption thresholds.

## Limitation

Applies on top of income tax for high earners and in the mitigation phase-in zone.
