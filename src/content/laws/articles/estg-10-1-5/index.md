# Childcare Expenses Special Deduction (Kinderbetreuungskosten)

Allows deducting qualified daycare, Kita, Tagesmutter, or nanny care expenses as Sonderausgaben for children under age 14: two-thirds up to €4,000/child through 2024, raised to 80% up to €4,800/child/year from 2025 under JStG 2024.

## Benefit

Lowers annual joint taxable income (zvE) by up to €4,800 per child under 14 from 2025 onward (80% of €6,000 paid care expenses).

## Limitation

Covers pure care contributions only — meal charges and sports/music tuition remain non-deductible, and cash reimbursement requires verified invoices plus bank transfer.
