# KfW 300 Home Ownership for Families (Wohneigentum für Familien)

KfW 300 offers a subsidized loan to eligible families with at least 1 minor child for a self-occupied, climate-friendly new build (EH 40) or qualifying first purchase in Germany.

## Benefit

Provides reduced-interest housing loans up to:

- **1–2 minor children**: €170,000 (EH 40) or €220,000 with QNG-Plus
- **3–4 minor children**: €200,000 (EH 40) or €250,000 with QNG-Plus
- **5+ minor children**: €220,000 (EH 40) or €270,000 with QNG-Plus

Household taxable income (zvE, 2-year average from the 2nd and 3rd calendar years before application) must stay at or below **€90,000 for 1 child + €10,000 per additional child**. Standard repayment options include 10–35 year annuity loans with 1–5 interest-only startup years (*Tilgungsfreie Anlaufjahre*) or 10-year bullet loans (*Endfälliges Darlehen*), typically with a 10-year fixed-rate period.

## Limitation

KfW checks additional conditions that this calculator does not verify (including no existing residential property ownership in Germany, technical confirmation by an energy-efficiency expert, and application via a financing partner bank prior to signing construction/purchase contracts). Interest rates are updated periodically by KfW; always replace illustrative simulator defaults with your current bank offer.
