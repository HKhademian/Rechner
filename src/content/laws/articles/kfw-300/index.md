# KfW 300 Home Ownership for Families (Wohneigentum für Familien)

KfW 300 offers a subsidized loan to eligible families with at least 1 minor child for a self-occupied, climate-friendly new build (EH 40) or qualifying first purchase (within 12 months of *Bauabnahme*) in Germany.

## Benefit

Provides reduced-interest housing loans up to:

- **1–2 minor children**: €170,000 (EH 40) or €220,000 with QNG-Plus / QNG-Premium
- **3–4 minor children**: €200,000 (EH 40) or €250,000 with QNG
- **5+ minor children**: €220,000 (EH 40) or €270,000 with QNG

Household taxable income (zvE, 2-year average from the 2nd and 3rd calendar years before application) must stay at or below **€90,000 for 1 child + €10,000 per additional child**. Official repayment tiers (10-year fixed rate; Nov 2026 reference rates): 4–10 yr annuity (1–2 yr grace, 0.01%), 11–25 yr annuity (1–3 yr grace, 0.51%), 26–35 yr annuity (1–5 yr grace, 0.78%), or 4–10 yr bullet loan (*Endfälliges Darlehen*, 0.93%). Combinable with KfW 124 (up to €100,000) and commercial bank loans.

## Limitation

Requires ≥50% co-ownership, 0% prior residential property ownership in Germany, no prior *Baukindergeld* (KfW 424), no oil/gas/biomass heating, technical confirmation (*BzA*/*BnD*) by a dena energy-efficiency expert, and application via a financing partner bank before signing contracts. Partial prepayments (*Sondertilgung*) are not allowed; a 0.15%/month commitment fee applies after 12 months on undrawn funds; and market follow-up rates apply after the 10-year *Zinsbindung*. For existing homes, see sister program KfW 308 (*Jung kauft Alt*).
