# KfW 300 Home Ownership for Families (Wohneigentum für Familien)

KfW 300 offers a subsidized loan to eligible families for a self-occupied, climate-friendly new build or qualifying first purchase in Germany. Income, household, ownership and technical conditions apply.

## Benefit

Provides subsidized housing loans for energy-efficient homes when zvE stays below the family threshold.

## Limitation

KfW checks additional conditions that this calculator does not verify. Income is based on the applicable tax assessments; the building must meet technical requirements confirmed by experts.
