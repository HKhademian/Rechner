# Income from Employment (Einkünfte aus nichtselbständiger Arbeit)

Classifies regular wages, salaries, bonuses, and employer supplements as taxable employment income.

## Benefit

Qualifies for the § 9a work-expense lump sum and standard payroll withholding.

## Limitation

Contractual bonuses and 13th-month salaries are taxed as ordinary employment income.
