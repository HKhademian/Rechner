# Household Services & Craftsmanship Tax Credit

Credits 20% of qualifying craftsman labor invoices directly against annual income tax liability, capped at €1,200/year (€6,000 labor).

## Benefit

Direct euro-for-euro income tax reduction up to €1,200/year for home renovation and maintenance labor.

## Limitation

Material costs and cash payments are excluded; requires bank-transfer invoice verification.
