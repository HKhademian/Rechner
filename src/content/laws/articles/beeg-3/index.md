# Offsetting Maternity & Wage-Replacement Benefits

Credits maternity benefits (Mutterschaftsgeld and employer supplement) euro-for-euro against Elterngeld in overlapping months.

## Benefit

Ensures maternity pay covers 100% of prior net without consuming extra family Basis months beyond the offset.

## Limitation

Maternity months still count as Basiselterngeld months used by the mother and zero out simultaneous Elterngeld payouts.
