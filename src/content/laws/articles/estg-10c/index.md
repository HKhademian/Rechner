# Special-Expense Lump Sum (Sonderausgaben-Pauschbetrag)

Provides a small automatic special-expense allowance (€36 single / €72 joint) when no higher itemized special expenses are claimed.

## Benefit

Automatically reduces taxable income by €36 (€72 joint) without documentation.

## Limitation

Modest fixed amount that does not scale with income.
