# Kindergeld Personal Entitlement Conditions

Establishes residency, parental relationship, and personal eligibility criteria for monthly Kindergeld.

## Benefit

Covers biological, adopted, and qualifying household children in Germany.

## Limitation

Requires valid German residency/tax-status ties and ongoing child qualification.
