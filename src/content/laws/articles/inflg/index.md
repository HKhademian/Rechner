# Bracket Creep Relief & Inflation Adjustment (Inflationsausgleich)

Periodically shifts § 32a EStG tariff brackets, Grundfreibetrag, and Kinderfreibetrag upward to neutralize cold progression and model future statutory inflation indexing.

## Benefit

Prevents inflation-driven nominal wage increases from pushing households into higher real income tax brackets.

## Limitation

Unlegislated future years beyond verified statutory tables (2026+) depend on optional projection assumptions rather than fixed law.
