# Statutory 20-Year Solar Feed-In Tariff (Einspeisevergütung)

Guarantees fixed ct/kWh feed-in tariffs for surplus (Teileinspeisung) or full export (Volleinspeisung) over 20 calendar years, tiered at <= 10 kWp and 10–40 kWp.

## Benefit

Guarantees stable 20-year grid export revenues alongside ~37 ct/kWh avoided retail grid purchases.

## Limitation

Semi-annual 1% tariff degression reduces rates slightly for later commissioning dates.
