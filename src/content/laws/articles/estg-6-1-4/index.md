# Company Car Private Use & Electric Vehicle Basis Reduction

Sets monthly taxable private-use valuation at 1% of gross list price (0.5% PHEV; 0.25% pure EV up to the statutory price cap).

## Benefit

Pure electric company cars up to €100,000 gross list price pay tax on only one-quarter of the list price.

## Limitation

Vehicles above the €100,000 BEV cap drop from 0.25% to 0.5%; ICE vehicles carry the full 1.0% taxable uplift.
