# Commuter Distance Allowance (Entfernungspauschale) & Homeoffice Deduction

Deducts fixed per-kilometer work-commute allowances per office day (2024–2025: €0.30/km for first 20 km and €0.38/km from km 21; 2026+: €0.38/km from km 1; €4,500 non-car cap) plus up to €6/day (€1,260/yr) Homeoffice-Pauschale as income-related expenses.

## Benefit

Lowers annual taxable income once total Werbungskosten exceed the €1,230 Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag, or via actual public-transit fares under § 9 Abs. 2 S. 2 EStG.

## Limitation

Does not yield additional tax savings until total Werbungskosten exceed the automatic €1,230 lump sum, and tax-free § 3 Nr. 15 JobTicket subsidies reduce distance claims euro for euro.
