# Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte) Points System

Permits 1-year job-seeking entry in Germany via recognized degree OR 6 statutory points covering qualifications, experience, German/English level, and age, plus €1,091/mo living funds.

## Benefit

Allows international specialists without a pre-signed job contract to search on-site and work up to 20h/week part-time.

## Limitation

Requires proof of secured monthly livelihood (typically blocked account or declaration of commitment).
