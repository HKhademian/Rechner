# Fast-Track Permanent Settlement (Niederlassungserlaubnis)

EU Blue Card holders qualify for permanent settlement after 27 months with basic A1 German, or after 21 months with B1 German proficiency.

## Benefit

Shortens permanent residency wait from 60 months to 21 months (B1 German) or 27 months (A1 German).

## Limitation

Requires uninterrupted qualified employment and compulsory statutory pension contributions throughout the period.
