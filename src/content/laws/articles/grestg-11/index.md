# Real Estate Transfer Tax (Grunderwerbsteuer)

Applies state-specific real estate transfer tax (3.5% in Bavaria to 6.5% in NRW, Brandenburg, Saarland, Schleswig-Holstein) on residential property purchases.

## Benefit

Exemptions apply to close direct-line family transfers (§ 3 Nr. 4/6 GrEStG).

## Limitation

Closing taxes plus notary/registry and agent fees cannot typically be financed with equity-free mortgages.
