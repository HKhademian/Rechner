# EU Blue Card (Blaue Karte EU)

Primary German work permit for university graduates earning at least 50% of BBG RV (2026: €50,700) or 45.3% for shortage/recent graduates/IT (€45,934.20).

## Benefit

Unlocks spouse work authorization without German tests and accelerated permanent residency under § 18c AufenthG.

## Limitation

Annual salary adjustments are reassessed at renewal against updated BBG-indexed thresholds.
