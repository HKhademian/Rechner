# Election of Individual vs. Joint Assessment

Allows married spouses or registered civil partners to elect either individual assessment or joint assessment.

## Benefit

Gives couples flexibility to choose joint splitting or separate assessment when beneficial.

## Limitation

Joint assessment is never automatic from living together; it requires marriage/registered partnership and explicit election.
