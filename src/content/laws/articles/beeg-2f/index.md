# Standardized BEEG Social Security Flat Deductions

Deducts flat statutory percentages (9% health/care, 10% pension, 2% unemployment) without ordinary contribution ceilings, plus a transition-zone factor.

## Benefit

Applies a lower transition-zone deduction factor for low-to-mid incomes.

## Limitation

High earners have no upper contribution ceiling in BEEG net (unlike real payroll), reducing BEEG net compared to actual take-home pay.
