# Child Allowance (Kinderfreibetrag & BEA)

Sets the annual tax-free allowance per child (subsistence + care/education/training allowance), prorated by birth month.

## Benefit

Protects the minimum subsistence cost of each child from income tax and reduces SolZ/church tax.

## Limitation

Only lowers income tax if its tax relief exceeds annual Kindergeld under § 31 Günstigerprüfung.
