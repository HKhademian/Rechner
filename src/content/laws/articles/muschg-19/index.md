# Statutory Maternity Allowance (Mutterschaftsgeld)

Provides the health-insurer share of maternity pay (up to €13/calendar day for statutory insured employees) during § 3 protection periods.

## Benefit

Tax-free base maternity contribution paired with the employer supplement.

## Limitation

Offsets Elterngeld under § 3 BEEG and enters § 32b EStG progression.
