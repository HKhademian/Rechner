# Tax-Free Employer JobTicket & Public Transit Subsidies

Exempts employer-paid public transit and Deutschlandticket subsidies provided in addition to contractual salary from income tax and social security, while offsetting the commuter distance allowance.

## Benefit

Delivers 100% tax-free and social-contribution-free commuting support when paid on top of agreed salary.

## Limitation

Reduces the employee's claimable § 9 Abs. 1 Nr. 4 Entfernungspauschale euro-for-euro by the tax-free subsidy amount.
