# Monthly Child Benefit Amount (Kindergeld)

Sets the uniform monthly tax-exempt child benefit payment per qualifying child (€259/month in 2026).

## Benefit

Provides steady, tax-free monthly cash per child regardless of parent income.

## Limitation

Credited against the § 32 Kinderfreibetrag tax advantage during annual § 31 Günstigerprüfung.
