# Claim Duration, Parent Budgets & Partner Months

Allocates 12 Basis months plus 2 additional Partner months (14 total), subject to a minimum 2-month claim per claiming parent.

## Benefit

Grants 2 bonus Partner months when both parents claim at least 2 months each.

## Limitation

Claims shorter than 2 life-months are forfeited, and parallel Basis months by both parents have statutory overlap limits.
