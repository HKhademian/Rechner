# Care Insurance Child Surcharge & Child Discounts

Adds a +0.6% surcharge for childless employees aged 23+ and grants -0.25% per qualifying child (children 2 through 5 under age 25).

## Benefit

Parents with 2–5 children under 25 save up to 1.0 percentage point on long-term care contributions.

## Limitation

Childless employees aged 23+ pay an unshared +0.6% surcharge, and discounts expire as children turn 25.
