# Single-Parent Tax Relief (Entlastungsbetrag für Alleinerziehende)

Grants €4,260 relief (plus €240 per additional qualifying child) to eligible single parents in Tax Class II.

## Benefit

Significantly lowers Tax Class II withholding and annual taxable income for qualifying single parents.

## Limitation

Lost immediately if another adult shares the household registration (no true single-parent household).
