# Mixed & Self-Employed Income Rules

Coordinates assessment periods when self-employment or business income coincides with employment earnings.

## Benefit

Prevents double counting and sets deterministic rules when non-employment income is present.

## Limitation

Self-employed or mixed income shifts the assessment period to the last concluded tax assessment year rather than 12 pre-birth months.
