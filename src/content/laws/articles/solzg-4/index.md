# Solidarity Surcharge Rate, Exemption & Mitigation Zone

Applies 5.5% surcharge above the exemption threshold (doubled for joint spouses), phased in smoothly via the 11.9% mitigation zone.

## Benefit

Exempts income tax below €19,950 (single) / €39,900 (joint) in 2025, rising in 2026.

## Limitation

The 11.9% phase-in mitigation zone temporarily creates a higher effective marginal tax rate.
