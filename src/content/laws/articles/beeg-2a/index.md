# Sibling Bonus (Geschwisterbonus) & Multiple-Birth Addition

Increases positive Elterngeld by 10% (at least €75/month Basis or €37.50/month Plus) when qualifying younger siblings live in the household.

## Benefit

Adds +10% (min €75 Basis / €37.50 Plus) when 1 older child is under 3 or 2+ older children are under 6.

## Limitation

Applies only while the older sibling stays under the age threshold and adds nothing if the base allowance is zero.
