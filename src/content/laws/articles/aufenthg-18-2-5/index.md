# Age 45+ Skilled Worker Pension-Adequacy Salary Floor

Applicants receiving their first skilled work residence permit at age 45 or older must earn at least 55% of the statutory pension ceiling or prove adequate retirement provision.

## Benefit

Protects older incoming professionals from old-age poverty risk through an objective salary or pension-proof gate.

## Limitation

First-time applicants aged 45+ below €55,770 gross/yr (2026) must document separate private pension assets.
