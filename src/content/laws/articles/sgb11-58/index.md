# Long-Term Care Employer/Employee Split & Saxony Rule

Splits the standard care insurance rate equally between employer and employee outside Saxony, while Saxony shifts an extra 0.5% to employees.

## Benefit

Equal 50/50 base rate sharing across 15 German federal states.

## Limitation

Employees working in Saxony pay an additional 0.5% employee care contribution.
