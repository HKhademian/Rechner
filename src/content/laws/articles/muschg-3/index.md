# Maternity Protection Windows (Mutterschutzfristen)

Prohibits employment during the statutory protection window (6 weeks before expected birth and 8 weeks after birth, 12 weeks for premature/multiple births).

## Benefit

Protects health and triggers full net wage replacement alongside § 2b BEEG exclusion protection.

## Limitation

Calendar-month simulations approximate day-exact 6/8-week windows; premature or multiple births extend post-birth windows.
