# Statutory Unemployment Insurance Contribution Rate

Sets the total statutory unemployment insurance contribution rate (2.6%, split equally 1.3% employee / 1.3% employer) up to the pension assessment ceiling.

## Benefit

Funds unemployment protection and is capped at the annual pension contribution ceiling.

## Limitation

Employee share may be crowded out of § 10 EStG tax deductibility once health/care premiums exceed the other-insurance limit.
