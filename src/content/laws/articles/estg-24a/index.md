# Age Relief Allowance (Altersentlastungsbetrag)

Grants an age-cohort percentage relief (with an annual euro cap) on positive wages for taxpayers over age 64 on January 1.

## Benefit

Reduces annual taxable income automatically based on the permanent birth-year cohort.

## Limitation

Cohort percentage and euro cap phase down over time and require known age at start of year.
