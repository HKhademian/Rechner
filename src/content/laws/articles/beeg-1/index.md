# Elterngeld Eligibility, Working Hours Cap & Income Limit

Establishes personal eligibility for Elterngeld, the 32-hour weekly working limit during allowance receipt, and the couple taxable-income eligibility cap (§ 1 Abs. 8 BEEG).

## Benefit

Protects parental allowance eligibility while working part-time up to 32 hours per week.

## Limitation

Exceeding 32 weekly hours or surpassing the prior-year household taxable income cap (€175,000 from April 2025) eliminates entitlement completely.
