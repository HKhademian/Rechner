# Midijob Transition Zone (Übergangsbereich)

Reduces employee social-security contributions smoothly for monthly regular wages between the Minijob threshold and €2,000 while preserving full pension entitlements.

## Benefit

Significantly increases net take-home pay for mid-low and part-time salaries without losing pension credits.

## Limitation

Does not cover special trainee/student or multiple-job scenarios automatically and interacts specially with care surcharges.
