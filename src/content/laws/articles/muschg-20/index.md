# Employer Maternity Supplement (Arbeitgeberzuschuss)

Requires employers to top up § 19 Mutterschaftsgeld to 100% of the average net wage of the last 3 completed calendar months before protection starts.

## Benefit

Maintains 100% of pre-maternity net pay during the protection window without payroll tax withholding.

## Limitation

Because it uses the last 3 months before maternity, a late pay cut lowers it, and it still counts toward § 32b progression.
