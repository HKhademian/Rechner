# Residential Photovoltaic Income Tax Exemption

Exempts income and self-consumption withdrawals from small photovoltaic installations (up to 30 kWp on single-family homes) from German income tax.

## Benefit

Eliminates income-tax reporting and EÜR profit taxation on qualifying rooftop solar earnings.

## Limitation

Does not shield installations exceeding statutory kWp ceilings per residential unit.
