# Capital Investment Income (Kapitalerträge)

Governs dividends, interest, and investment gains; excluded from BEEG employment earnings but relevant for § 2 Abs. 5a eligibility.

## Benefit

Allows tracking passive dividend/ETF cash flows separately from employment payroll.

## Limitation

Still counts toward the prior-year household taxable income cap for Elterngeld eligibility.
