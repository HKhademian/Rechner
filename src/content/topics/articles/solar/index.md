# Solar feed-in and tax treatment

A solar installation can supply household electricity and export surplus electricity to the grid. Feed-in compensation and tax treatment follow separate rules.

## Potential benefit

Self-consumption can reduce purchased electricity and eligible exported electricity can earn compensation.

## Conditions and limitations

Actual yield, tariffs, technical eligibility and tax treatment depend on the installation and commissioning details.

## In the calculators

The [Solar Savings Calculator](rechner://app/solar-energy) uses entered yield, consumption and costs with modeled tariffs and assumptions; it is not a tax assessment.
