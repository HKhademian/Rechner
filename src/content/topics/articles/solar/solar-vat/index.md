# Zero VAT on eligible solar installations

A zero VAT rate can apply to qualifying supplies and installation of photovoltaic systems under the statutory conditions.

## Potential benefit

Eligible purchases are invoiced at a zero VAT rate.

## Conditions and limitations

The installation and supply must meet the statutory conditions; the setting is not a general exemption for every energy purchase.

## In the calculators

The switch assumes the purchase qualifies when estimating system cost and payback.
