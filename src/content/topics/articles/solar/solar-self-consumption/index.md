# Solar self-consumption and feed-in

Self-consumption is the share of generated electricity used by the household; feed-in is electricity exported to the grid.

## Potential benefit

Self-consumed electricity can replace grid purchases; eligible exports can earn compensation.

## Conditions and limitations

The actual share depends on when electricity is generated and used, batteries and other system details.

## In the calculators

The entered share divides modeled generation between household use and exported electricity.
