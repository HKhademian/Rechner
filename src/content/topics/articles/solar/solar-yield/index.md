# Solar system size and specific yield

Kilowatt-peak (kWp) describes a solar system's rated peak power under standard test conditions. Specific yield estimates annual electricity generation per kWp, in kWh per kWp per year.

## Potential benefit

Together they give a simple estimate of annual generation.

## Conditions and limitations

Actual output varies with location, shading, orientation, weather and system performance.

## In the calculators

The estimate multiplies system size by specific yield; it is not a site survey.
