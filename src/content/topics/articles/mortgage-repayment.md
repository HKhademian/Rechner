# Mortgage financing and repayment

Mortgage interest is the cost of borrowing; initial repayment (Tilgung) is the part of the starting annual payment used to reduce the loan principal.

## Potential benefit

A higher repayment rate reduces the outstanding balance faster, all else being equal.

## Conditions and limitations

The interest rate, repayment schedule, fixed-rate period, fees and future refinancing can differ from this simple estimate.

## In the calculators

The home planner estimates the initial bank payment. The mortgage calculator projects a balance using fixed rates; neither is a complete loan offer.
