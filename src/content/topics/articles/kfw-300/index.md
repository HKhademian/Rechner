# KfW 300: family home loan

A [subsidized loan program](rechner://law/kfw-300) (*Wohneigentum für Familien – Neubau*) for eligible families building or first-buying (within 12 months of *Bauabnahme*) a climate-friendly new home (EH 40) to live in.

## Potential benefit

A qualifying household can finance part of the project at subsidized KfW rates (as of Nov 2026: **0.01%** for 4–10 yr, **0.51%** for 11–25 yr, **0.78%** for 26–35 yr, or **0.93%** for 4–10 yr balloon—all with up to 10-year fixed rates) instead of a ~3.6% commercial bank rate. This is a loan that must be repaid, not a grant. Maximum loan caps are €170,000 for 1–2 minor children (€220,000 with QNG-Plus/Premium), €200,000 for 3–4 children (€250,000 with QNG), and €220,000 for 5+ children (€270,000 with QNG). It can be combined with KfW 124 (up to €100,000) and commercial bank mortgages.

## Conditions and limitations

Household taxable income (*zu versteuerndes Einkommen*, zvE, 2-year average from the 2nd and 3rd calendar years prior to application—e.g. 2023 & 2024 tax assessments for 2026) must stay at or below €90,000 for 1 minor child plus €10,000 per additional child. Requires ≥50% co-ownership, 0% prior residential property ownership in Germany, no prior *Baukindergeld* (KfW 424), EH 40 with no oil/gas/biomass heating, and a dena energy-efficiency expert (*BzA* before signing contracts). Partial prepayments (*Sondertilgung*) are not allowed, and a 0.15%/month commitment fee applies after 12 months on undrawn funds. For existing (used) homes, see sister program KfW 308 (*Jung kauft Alt*).
