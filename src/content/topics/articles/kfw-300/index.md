# KfW 300: family home loan

A [subsidized loan program](rechner://law/kfw-300) (*Wohneigentum für Familien*) for eligible families building or buying a qualifying climate-friendly new home (EH 40) to live in.

## Potential benefit

A qualifying household can finance a large share of the project at a subsidized KfW interest rate instead of a commercial bank rate; this is a loan that must be repaid, not a grant. Maximum KfW 300 loan caps are €170,000 for 1–2 minor children (€220,000 with QNG-Plus), €200,000 for 3–4 children (€250,000 with QNG-Plus), and €220,000 for 5+ children (€270,000 with QNG-Plus).

## Conditions and limitations

Household taxable income (*zu versteuerndes Einkommen*, zvE, 2-year average from the 2nd and 3rd calendar years prior to application) must stay at or below €90,000 for 1 minor child plus €10,000 for each additional minor child. Ownership restrictions (no existing residential property in Germany) and EH 40 / QNG building standards confirmed by an energy-efficiency expert also apply.
