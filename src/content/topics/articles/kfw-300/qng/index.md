# QNG sustainability certification

The Quality Seal for Sustainable Buildings (Qualitätssiegel Nachhaltiges Gebäude, QNG) certifies sustainability. KfW 300 accepts the PLUS or PREMIUM tier for its higher funding level.

## Potential benefit

The QNG funding tier can allow a higher KfW loan ceiling.

## Conditions and limitations

A sustainability certificate, expert confirmation and the program's other building and household conditions are required.

## In the calculators

The switch adds the configured QNG top-up to the potential loan ceiling, subject to modeled eligibility and financing need.
