# Owner-occupied EH 40 new build

Owner-occupied means you live in the home yourself. Efficiency House 40 (EH 40) is an energy-efficiency standard for a building.

## Potential benefit

The standard is one building requirement for the KfW 300 climate-friendly housing tiers.

## Conditions and limitations

KfW also requires lifecycle emissions limits, prohibits oil, gas or biomass heating, and requires expert confirmation. The first-purchase and household conditions also apply.

## In the calculators

The switch is an assumption that the self-occupied home meets the program's building requirements; the calculator does not verify them.
