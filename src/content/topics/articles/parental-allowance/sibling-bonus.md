# Elterngeld sibling bonus

The sibling bonus (Geschwisterbonus) is an addition to parental allowance for a qualifying family with older children.

## Potential benefit

It increases the allowance while the statutory conditions are satisfied.

## Conditions and limitations

The number and ages of the children and other eligibility conditions matter.

## In the calculators

The planner uses the modeled family and birth timeline to estimate whether the bonus applies.
