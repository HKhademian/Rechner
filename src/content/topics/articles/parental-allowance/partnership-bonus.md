# Elterngeld partnership bonus

The partnership bonus provides additional ElterngeldPlus months when eligible parents share work and childcare and satisfy the applicable working-time conditions.

## Potential benefit

Qualifying parents can receive additional months of ElterngeldPlus.

## Conditions and limitations

Both parents must meet the relevant requirements over the claimed period; the exact rules depend on the family's circumstances.

## In the calculators

The planner uses the entered work schedule to model the bonus, without verifying the official claim.
