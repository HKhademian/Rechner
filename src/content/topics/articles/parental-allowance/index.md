# Parental allowance and parental leave

Elterngeld is an income-related family benefit; Elternzeit is leave from employment. They are related but separate arrangements.

## Potential benefit

Eligible parents can receive financial support while caring for a child; different allowance options can be combined under their rules.

## Conditions and limitations

Income, working hours, timing and other conditions affect eligibility and amounts. A simulation is not an official determination.

The [Parental Leave Planner](rechner://app/parental-leave) estimates payments and cash flow from the entered household and leave timeline.
