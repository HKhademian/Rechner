# Basiselterngeld and ElterngeldPlus

Basiselterngeld and ElterngeldPlus are different ways to receive parental allowance. ElterngeldPlus can spread an entitlement over a longer period and is often relevant when working part-time.

## Potential benefit

The options allow families to plan benefit timing around work and childcare.

## Conditions and limitations

Monthly amounts, claim periods and combinations depend on income, working time and the parents' situation.

## In the calculators

The parental planner estimates monthly payments from the selected claim and work timeline.
