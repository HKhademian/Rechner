# Kindergeld and child tax allowances

Kindergeld is a child benefit. Child tax allowances are considered separately in the annual income-tax assessment.

## Potential benefit

Eligible families can receive child benefit and may receive additional tax relief when the statutory comparison is favorable.

## Conditions and limitations

The assessment coordinates child benefit and allowance relief; they are not simply two full independent payments.

## In the calculators

The calculators use the modeled child count and assumptions; a complete individual eligibility assessment is not performed.
