# GiroCode and SEPA credit transfers

A GiroCode is a scannable payment symbol containing payment instructions in a format supported by participating banking apps. A SEPA credit transfer is a euro bank transfer under the SEPA scheme.

## Potential benefit

Scanning can fill the recipient and transfer details in a banking app, reducing manual typing.

## Conditions and limitations

The code does not send money or verify the recipient. Check the details in your banking app before approving a transfer; support varies by bank.

## In the calculators

The [GiroCode Generator](rechner://app/girocode) creates a payment code locally from the entered transfer details. It does not submit the transfer.
