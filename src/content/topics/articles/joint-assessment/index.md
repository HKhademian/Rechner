# Joint assessment and splitting

Eligible spouses or registered partners can be assessed jointly. The splitting method calculates income tax using a shared tax base.

## Potential benefit

It can lower combined income tax compared with individual assessment, depending on each partner's income.

## Conditions and limitations

Eligibility and individual circumstances matter; payroll withholding classes do not themselves determine the final annual tax.

## In the calculators

The joint setting applies the modeled splitting calculation to the estimate.
