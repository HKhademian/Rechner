# Nursing-care insurance contributions

Social nursing-care insurance contributions depend on factors including parent status, children's ages and the place of employment.

## Potential benefit

Applicable child-related adjustments can reduce the employee contribution.

## Conditions and limitations

The qualifying details and evidence matter. Saxony has a different employee/employer allocation.

## In the calculators

The calculator uses the entered parent and child details and Saxony setting to estimate the employee share.
