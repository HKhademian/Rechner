# Opportunity Card points factors

The points route considers qualifications, experience, language, age, previous stays and a qualifying partner. A shortage occupation is a profession in the applicable shortage list; partial recognition means an authority has identified differences from the German qualification. The prior-stay and partner factors have their own conditions.

## Potential benefit

Qualifying factors can contribute toward the points needed for the points-based route.

## Conditions and limitations

Each factor has its own conditions and evidence requirements; a calculated score alone does not establish eligibility.

## In the calculators

The switches reflect factors assumed by this model and are not verification of the supporting documents.
