# Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)

The Opportunity Card is a residence route for job seeking. Depending on qualifications, applicants may use a recognized qualification route or a points-based route.

## Potential benefit

It can provide time in Germany to seek qualifying employment when the requirements are met.

## Conditions and limitations

The points total is only part of the assessment; qualification, language, financial support and other requirements may apply.

## In the calculators

The visa tool scores the entered factors and estimates stay costs; it does not verify documents or grant a permit.
