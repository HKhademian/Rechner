# EU Blue Card

The EU Blue Card is a residence permit for qualifying skilled employment, with requirements that depend on the applicant and job.

## Potential benefit

It provides a route to work and residence in Germany and may offer a route to settlement subject to additional conditions.

## Conditions and limitations

Recognition, occupation, salary and other requirements must be checked with the responsible authority; different thresholds can apply.

## In the calculators

The visa tool compares the supplied salary and occupation track with modeled thresholds; it does not decide permit eligibility.
