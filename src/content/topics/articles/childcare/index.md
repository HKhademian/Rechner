# Childcare costs and tax deduction

Qualifying childcare expenses can be considered as special expenses for income tax under the applicable conditions.

## Potential benefit

An eligible deduction can reduce taxable income and therefore the family's tax bill.

## Conditions and limitations

Not every expense qualifies: meals and the particular payment and documentation requirements must be considered. The deduction is not a euro-for-euro refund.
