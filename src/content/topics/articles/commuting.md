# Commuting allowance and JobTicket

The distance allowance is a work-expense deduction for commuting. An employer-supported JobTicket has separate tax treatment that can affect the deduction.

## Potential benefit

Eligible work expenses may reduce taxable income; an eligible employer ticket can reduce the employee's travel cost.

## Conditions and limitations

The tax saving depends on the overall deduction and tax position. An employer subsidy must be taken into account; a deduction is not a refund of its full amount.

## In the calculators

The tool compares modeled self-paid and employer-ticket scenarios using the entered costs and tax assumptions.
