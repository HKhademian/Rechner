# Payroll tax classes and ELStAM

A tax class is part of the electronic payroll tax information (ELStAM) used by an employer to withhold wage tax.

## Potential benefit

The class tailors withholding to the employee's registered circumstances.

## Conditions and limitations

Withholding is an advance, not necessarily the final annual income tax; changing class may also affect certain benefits.

## In the calculators

The selected class changes modeled payroll withholding in the [Net Pay Calculator](rechner://app/net-pay) and, where relevant, the parental allowance estimate.
