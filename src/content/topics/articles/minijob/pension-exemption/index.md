# Minijob pension insurance exemption

A Minijob employee can apply for exemption from compulsory pension insurance. The employer contribution and the employee's pension position are separate matters.

## Potential benefit

Exemption can increase current take-home pay by removing the modeled employee pension contribution.

## Conditions and limitations

Exemption affects compulsory pension-insurance coverage and entitlements; its consequences depend on the person's circumstances.

## In the calculators

The switch removes the modeled employee pension contribution from the Minijob calculation.
