# Minijob and Midijob

Minijob and Midijob are employment categories with different earnings limits and social-insurance treatment.

## Potential benefit

The transition-area rules can reduce an eligible employee's contribution burden; Minijobs have separate contribution and tax rules.

## Conditions and limitations

Classification, multiple jobs, pension-insurance choices and tax arrangements can change the result.

## In the calculators

The [Minijob & Midijob Calculator](rechner://app/minijob-midijob) estimates employee deductions using the selected pay, tax and insurance assumptions.
