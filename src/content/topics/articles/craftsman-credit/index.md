# Household craftsman tax credit

Certain labor costs for qualifying work in an existing household may qualify for an income-tax reduction.

## Potential benefit

An eligible credit reduces income tax subject to statutory limits.

## Conditions and limitations

Qualifying work, labor costs, invoices and payment rules matter. It is not a general subsidy for all new-build or material costs.

## In the calculators

The home planner applies the modeled credit to the entered annual eligible labor cost; it does not verify the work or tax liability.
