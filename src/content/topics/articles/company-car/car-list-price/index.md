# Gross list price for a company car

Bruttolistenpreis is the vehicle's gross domestic list price used as an input to the flat-rate valuation of private car use, rather than necessarily the amount paid for the car.

## Potential benefit

The list-price method offers a standardized way to estimate the taxable non-cash benefit.

## Conditions and limitations

Applicable rounding, eligible equipment, powertrain and valuation conditions matter.

## In the calculators

The company-car tool applies modeled valuation percentages to the entered list price.
