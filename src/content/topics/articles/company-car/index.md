# Company car taxable benefit

Private use of an employer-provided car can be treated as a taxable non-cash benefit. Valuation can depend on the car and commuting method.

## Potential benefit

Eligible electric vehicles may use reduced valuation rates under applicable rules.

## Conditions and limitations

Actual payroll treatment depends on vehicle eligibility, use and the chosen valuation method; a taxable benefit is not the same as cash received.

## In the calculators

The [Company Car Calculator](rechner://app/company-car) estimates the taxable amount and applies the entered marginal rate to approximate net cost.
