# Company car commute valuation

A company car used for trips between home and the first workplace can generate an additional taxable benefit. The valuation can use a monthly method or a method based on actual trips, subject to the rules.

## Potential benefit

The available method can affect the amount added to taxable pay.

## Conditions and limitations

Actual use, employer payroll practice and documentation requirements matter.

## In the calculators

Choose the modeled method and enter one-way distance and workplace days to compare the estimated amount.
