# Qualification recognition

Recognition compares a foreign qualification with the relevant German qualification. A notice can identify substantial differences rather than full equivalence.

## Potential benefit

Recognition or partial recognition may support different skilled-worker or Opportunity Card routes.

## Conditions and limitations

The competent authority decides recognition. A degree, professional experience or a points estimate alone does not establish equivalence.

## In the calculators

Choose the appropriate modeled recognition condition; the tool does not check the actual notice or qualification.
