# Maternity protection and pay

Mutterschutz is a legal protection period around childbirth; maternity benefits and employer payments can replace part of earnings during eligible periods.

## Potential benefit

It provides employment protection and, where eligible, financial support around birth.

## Conditions and limitations

Eligibility and payment sources depend on employment, insurance and individual circumstances; maternity benefits can affect parental allowance for the same period.

The parental planner estimates maternity cash flow and its interaction with modeled parental allowance.
