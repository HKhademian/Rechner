# Taxable income (zvE)

Zu versteuerndes Einkommen (zvE) is the taxable income used as the basis for income tax after applicable adjustments and deductions; it is not the same as gross salary.

## Potential benefit

It provides the tax base for estimates and some income-dependent eligibility tests.

## Conditions and limitations

An estimate from salary inputs can differ from the amount on an official tax assessment. Programs may specify particular assessment years.

## In the calculators

Enter the relevant taxable income for the modeled household or program rather than gross pay.
