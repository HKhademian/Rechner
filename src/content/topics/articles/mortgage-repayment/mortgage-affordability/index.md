# Mortgage affordability estimate

An affordability estimate compares your household's income after tax with spending, home running costs and loan payments.

## Potential benefit

It shows the cash remaining each month under the amounts and loan terms you enter.

## Conditions and limitations

It is not a lender's approval or a guarantee. Costs, income, rates and KfW eligibility can change, and a future lump sum needs its own funding.

## In the calculators

The simulator subtracts entered expenses, home costs and modeled loan payments. Its estimated purchase budget assumes fixed purchase costs, the entered KfW amount and all remaining cash are available; include any desired buffer in expenses.
