# Bank loan amount

The amount you plan to borrow from a bank for the purchase. It is the starting balance you must repay, before any interest or payments.

## Potential benefit

Entering the amount makes it possible to estimate the bank payment, interest and remaining balance over time.

## Conditions and limitations

This amount excludes the separately entered KfW loan. The bank loan is borrowed money, not the price of the home or the combined total of all loans. [KfW 300](rechner://topic/kfw-300) is one example of a KfW loan program.

## In the calculators

The mortgage simulator uses this value for the bank repayment and adds any KfW amount to total debt and loan-to-value. It is an independent input: changing the property price, savings or purchase costs here does not recalculate the loan amount. The Home Purchase Planner can calculate and transfer an estimate.
