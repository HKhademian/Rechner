# Purchase costs beyond the property price

The extra one-time costs of buying a home, such as property transfer tax, notary and land registry fees, and any buyer's estate-agent fee. These are in addition to the property's purchase price.

## Potential benefit

Including these costs helps you estimate the total cash needed to buy, instead of planning for the property price alone.

## Conditions and limitations

The amount depends on the property, location and transaction. An estimate is not a bill, and may not include every expense.

## In the calculators

In the mortgage simulator this amount is used in the estimated purchase budget; changing it does not automatically change the separately entered bank loan. The Home Purchase Planner can calculate and transfer an estimate.
