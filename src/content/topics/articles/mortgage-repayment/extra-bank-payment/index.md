# Extra bank principal payment

An extra principal payment is money paid in addition to the regular bank installment to reduce the outstanding bank loan.

## Potential benefit

If permitted and paid consistently, it can reduce future interest and bring the bank payoff date forward.

## Conditions and limitations

Your agreement may restrict extra repayments or charge fees. This option does not pay down the separate KfW loan.

## In the calculators

The simulator adds the entered amount to each bank payment until the bank loan is paid off and compares it with the same scenario without the extra payments.
