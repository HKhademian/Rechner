# Target bank payoff term

The payoff term is the time over which you want to repay the bank loan in full through regular monthly payments.

## Potential benefit

A longer term usually reduces the regular monthly payment; a shorter term generally means more principal is repaid sooner.

## Conditions and limitations

The payoff term is not necessarily the fixed-interest period. Actual rates can change when a fixed-rate period ends, and the lender's payment terms can differ.

## In the calculators

In payoff-term mode the simulator estimates a fixed monthly bank payment that would repay the loan over the selected term, assuming the rate stays unchanged. Extra bank payments can shorten the payoff time.
