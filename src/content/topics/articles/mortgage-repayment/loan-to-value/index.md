# Loan-to-value (LTV)

Loan-to-value compares what you borrow with the value of the property. For example, €400,000 of loans divided by a €500,000 property value is 80%.

## Potential benefit

It helps you see how much of the property's value is covered by debt.

## Conditions and limitations

A lender can use its own property valuation and lending rules. Purchase costs financed by loans can make the ratio higher than 100%.

## In the calculators

The simulator divides the starting bank loan plus the KfW loan by the property price or value you enter. Changing the value does not automatically change the loans.
