# Timeline Notation and Relative Pregnancy Counter

The relative month counter (`Mx`) in the timeline ledger must start relative to the **first pregnancy positive test**, NOT the birth of the first child:

1. **Origin ($M0$)**: The month containing the first positive pregnancy test is designated as `M0 (Pregnancy Positive)`.
2. **Pre-Pregnancy ($M-x$)**: Any planning months prior to the first positive pregnancy test are designated as negative offsets (`M-1`, `M-2`, ..., `M-8`).
3. **Post-Pregnancy ($M+x$)**: Months after the positive pregnancy test are labeled with positive offsets (`M+1`, `M+2`, ..., `M+8 (Birth 1)`, ..., `M+x`).
4. **Delivery Milestone**: Child 1 delivery typically occurs at `M+8 (Birth 1)` (8 months after positive pregnancy test at gestation week ~40).
